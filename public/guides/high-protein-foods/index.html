High protein, low calorie foods: the list that keeps you full

Updated 27 November 2026 · FitMe

When I was losing weight, the same five high protein foods went into my basket every week: 0% Greek yogurt, whey protein, chicken, tuna and eggs. Protein is what kept me full on fewer calories. This guide ranks 28 high protein, low calorie foods from the supermarket by how much protein you get per calorie, and shows how much you need.

Find my daily protein target

Why a high protein diet helps you lose weight

In a calorie deficit, you want the weight you lose to be fat, not muscle. Studies comparing diets with the same calories find that people eating more protein keep more muscle and lose more fat. Protein also helps you feel full, which makes eating less easier to stick to.

How much protein do you need to lose weight?

Research on weight loss points to about 1.2 to 1.6 g of protein per kg of body weight a day, with around 25 to 30 g at each meal. FitMe uses 1.5 g per kg of your goal weight. If your goal is 75 kg, that's about 110 g a day.

My five weekly protein staples

  • Greek yogurt 0%. 10 g of protein per 100 g for 59 kcal. Breakfast, snacks and creamy sauces, as in the chocolate protein yogurt.
  • Whey protein. The easiest 20 to 25 g of protein in the day. I had it every morning.
  • Chicken breast. The base of most of my lunches and dinners, like the chicken and egg fried rice.
  • Tuna in water. Cheap, keeps for months and needs no cooking. Try the creamy tuna pasta.
  • Eggs. Breakfast in five minutes, like the spinach and parmesan omelette.

My best trick: Greek yogurt, whey and berries

Stir a scoop of whey into a bowl of 0% Greek yogurt and top it with berries. It tastes like dessert, and it's one of the highest-protein snacks you can make for the calories. I had it at breakfast or as an afternoon snack.

Do you need protein powder?

No. Whey makes it easier, and it worked for me, but it's optional. Every FitMe recipe reaches its protein from normal food. The protein porridge, for example, gets 24 g from milk and Greek yogurt.

Watch the high protein foods that are high in calories

Peanut butter, almonds and parmesan have plenty of protein, but a lot of calories come with it. Use them in small amounts. In the plan, wherever a recipe uses peanut butter, swapping it for fruit gives you a bigger portion for fewer calories.

Questions people ask

Which food has the most protein per calorie? Of everyday supermarket foods, prawns, tuna in water, white fish like cod, and egg whites come out on top, at over 20 g of protein per 100 kcal.

Is 1.5 g per kg too much protein? For most healthy adults, no. It sits inside the range studied for weight loss. If you have kidney disease, talk to your doctor first.

Can I get enough protein as a vegetarian? Yes. Greek yogurt, cottage cheese, eggs, tofu, lentils and beans cover it. Every FitMe main meal has a vegetarian version, like the tofu curry.
