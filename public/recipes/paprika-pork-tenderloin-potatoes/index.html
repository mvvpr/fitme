Paprika pork tenderloin with roast potatoes and green beans

Pork tenderloin is one of the leanest cuts in the supermarket, and a smoked paprika rub gives it a crust without any frying. Everything roasts on one tray: 38 g of protein for about 460 calories, potatoes included.

Ingredients (1 portion)

  • 150 g pork tenderloin
  • 200 g baby potatoes
  • 120 g green beans
  • 2 tsp olive oil
  • 1 garlic clove
  • 1 tsp smoked paprika, dried oregano, salt and black pepper

Cooking for more people? Multiply everything. In your FitMe plan, the amounts are scaled to your own calories.

Method

  1. Heat the oven to 200°C (180°C fan). Halve the potatoes, toss with 1 tsp of the oil, salt and pepper and roast for 15 minutes.
  2. Mix the rest of the oil with the paprika, crushed garlic, oregano, salt and pepper and rub it all over the pork.
  3. Add the pork and the green beans to the tray and roast for 20 minutes, until the pork is cooked through and the juices run clear.
  4. Rest the pork for 5 minutes, slice it and serve with the potatoes and beans. Leftovers make a good cold lunch.

Where it fits in your day

At about 460 kcal, this is around a quarter of an 1,800-calorie day. Slice the leftovers cold for the next day's lunch.
