High protein beef and black bean chilli

Lean mince and black beans make a chilli that is filling without being heavy: 44 g of protein per portion, rice included. Smoked paprika and cumin do most of the work, so it tastes like it cooked for hours even though it takes 35 minutes.

Ingredients (1 portion)

  • 130 g lean beef mince (5% fat)
  • 80 g black beans (canned, drained)
  • 200 g chopped tomatoes (canned)
  • ½ onion
  • ½ bell pepper
  • 1 garlic clove
  • 50 g basmati rice (dry)
  • 1 tsp olive oil
  • 30 g greek yogurt 0%
  • 1 tsp ground cumin, 1 tsp smoked paprika, chilli powder to taste

Cooking for more people? Multiply everything. In your FitMe plan, the amounts are scaled to your own calories.

Method

  1. Rinse the rice and cook in 1½ times its volume of water, covered, for 12 minutes. Rest off the heat.
  2. Meanwhile, heat the oil and cook the diced onion and pepper for 5 minutes. Add the garlic and spices for 1 minute.
  3. Add the beef and brown for 5 minutes, breaking it up. Stir in the tomatoes and black beans and simmer for 15 minutes until thick.
  4. Serve over the rice with a spoonful of yogurt. It doubles well for batch cooking.

Where it fits in your day

At about 595 kcal, this chilli is roughly a third of an 1,800-calorie day. It is a good dinner after a long day, and doubling the batch gives you a ready meal for later in the week.
