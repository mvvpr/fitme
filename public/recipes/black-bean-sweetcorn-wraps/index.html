Black bean and sweetcorn wraps with tomato salsa

Mash about half the beans with a fork as they cook. The filling then holds together instead of rolling out of the wrap. With Greek yogurt and a quick tomato salsa, you get 26 g of protein for about 520 calories per portion, in 15 minutes.

Ingredients (1 portion)

  • 150 g black beans (canned, drained)
  • 1 wholemeal tortilla wrap
  • 50 g sweetcorn (canned, drained)
  • ½ bell pepper
  • ¼ onion
  • 1 tomato
  • 60 g greek yogurt 0%
  • 20 g mixed salad leaves
  • 1 tsp olive oil
  • 1 tsp ground cumin, 1 tsp smoked paprika, chilli powder to taste, salt

Cooking for more people? Multiply everything. In your FitMe plan, the amounts are scaled to your own calories.

Method

  1. Heat the oil in a frying pan and cook the sliced onion and pepper for 4 minutes.
  2. Add the black beans and the spices with 3 tbsp of water. Cook for 3 minutes, roughly mashing about half the beans with a fork so the filling holds together. Stir in the sweetcorn for the last minute.
  3. Dice the tomato and season it with a pinch of salt for a quick salsa. Warm the wrap in a dry pan for 20 seconds a side.
  4. Spread the yogurt over the wrap, add the salad leaves, the beans and the salsa, and roll up. For tomorrow's lunch, keep the filling in its own box and roll the wrap fresh.

Where it fits in your day

At about 520 kcal per portion, this is just over a quarter of an 1,800-calorie day. For tomorrow's lunch, pack the filling in its own box and roll the wrap fresh, so it doesn't go soggy.
