Apple slices with a peanut butter yogurt dip

Stirring the peanut butter into Greek yogurt stretches it into a whole bowl of dip and adds protein: 14 g per portion, for about 240 calories. Taking it to work? A squeeze of lemon on the slices stops them browning.

Time
3 min
kcal
240
protein
14 g
carbs
32 g
fat
8 g

per portion

Ingredients (1 portion)

  • 1 apple
  • 100 g greek yogurt 0%
  • 15 g peanut butter
  • Pinch of ground cinnamon

Cooking for more people? Multiply everything. In your FitMe plan, the amounts are scaled to your own calories.

Method

  1. Stir the peanut butter and cinnamon into the yogurt until smooth.
  2. Slice the apple and dip it in. A squeeze of lemon stops the slices browning if you pack it for later.
  3. Want a bigger snack? Leave out the peanut butter and have a second apple: about the same calories and twice the food.

Where it fits in your day

At about 240 kcal per portion, this is a filling afternoon snack. For more food at the same calories, skip the peanut butter and have a second apple: twice the snack, roughly the same calories.
