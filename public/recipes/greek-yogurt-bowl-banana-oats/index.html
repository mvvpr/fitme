Greek yogurt bowl with banana, oats and peanut butter

No cooking, five minutes, and 29 g of protein per portion, most of it from 200 g of Greek yogurt. Warm the peanut butter for 10 seconds so it drizzles. A thin line over the top goes a lot further than a spoonful stirred in.

Time
5 min
kcal
425
protein
29 g
carbs
58 g
fat
11 g

per portion

Ingredients (1 portion)

  • 200 g greek yogurt 0%
  • 30 g rolled oats
  • 1 banana
  • 15 g peanut butter
  • Pinch of ground cinnamon

Cooking for more people? Multiply everything. In your FitMe plan, the amounts are scaled to your own calories.

Method

  1. Spoon the yogurt into a bowl and slice the banana.
  2. Warm the peanut butter for 10 seconds in the microwave (or stir in a teaspoon of hot water) so it drizzles.
  3. Top the yogurt with the oats and banana, drizzle over the peanut butter and finish with the cinnamon.
  4. Want a bigger bowl? Swap the peanut butter for a big handful of berries (about 150 g): fewer calories and much more food.

Where it fits in your day

At about 425 kcal per portion, this bowl is roughly a quarter of an 1,800-calorie day. Want more food for fewer calories? Swap the peanut butter for a big handful of berries: fewer calories and a much bigger bowl.
