Black bean and lentil chilli with rice

Red lentils cook down into the sauce and make this chilli thick and filling without any meat. Together with black beans, they give you 31 g of protein per portion for about 595 calories, rice included. It doubles easily, so it is worth making a big pot.

Ingredients (1 portion)

  • 40 g red lentils (dry)
  • 100 g black beans (canned, drained)
  • 200 g chopped tomatoes (canned)
  • ½ onion
  • ½ bell pepper
  • 1 garlic clove
  • 50 g basmati rice (dry)
  • 1 tsp olive oil
  • 60 g greek yogurt 0%
  • 1 tsp ground cumin, 1 tsp smoked paprika, chilli powder to taste

Cooking for more people? Multiply everything. In your FitMe plan, the amounts are scaled to your own calories.

Method

  1. Rinse the rice and cook in 1½ times its volume of water, covered, for 12 minutes. Rest off the heat.
  2. Meanwhile, heat the oil and cook the diced onion and pepper for 5 minutes. Add the garlic and spices for 1 minute.
  3. Add the rinsed lentils, the tomatoes and 150 ml of water and simmer for 15 minutes until the lentils are soft. Stir in the black beans and cook 5 minutes more until thick, adding a splash of water if needed.
  4. Serve over the rice with a spoonful of yogurt. It doubles well for batch cooking.

Where it fits in your day

At about 595 kcal per portion, this is around a third of an 1,800-calorie day. Beans and lentils are high in fibre as well as protein, a combination that keeps you full for a long time in a calorie deficit.
