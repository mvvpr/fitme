Greek-style pork with couscous and yogurt sauce

Couscous only needs boiling water and five minutes, which makes this the fastest main in the plan. Lemon and oregano pork, a quick garlic yogurt and chopped tomato and cucumber give you 44 g of protein for about 480 calories.

Ingredients (1 portion)

  • 140 g pork tenderloin
  • 55 g wholewheat couscous (dry)
  • 80 g cherry tomatoes
  • 80 g cucumber
  • 60 g greek yogurt 0%
  • 10 ml lemon juice
  • 1 tsp olive oil
  • 1 garlic clove
  • Dried oregano, salt and black pepper, fresh parsley or mint (optional)

Cooking for more people? Multiply everything. In your FitMe plan, the amounts are scaled to your own calories.

Method

  1. Put the couscous in a bowl, pour over about 80 ml of boiling water, cover and leave for 5 minutes. Fluff it up with a fork.
  2. Slice the pork into 1 cm medallions and toss with half the lemon juice, half the crushed garlic, the oregano, salt and pepper.
  3. Heat the oil in a frying pan over a medium-high heat and cook the pork for about 3 minutes on each side, until cooked through.
  4. Mix the yogurt with the rest of the garlic and a pinch of salt. Chop the tomatoes and cucumber and stir them into the couscous with the rest of the lemon juice. Serve the pork on the couscous with the yogurt sauce.

Where it fits in your day

At about 480 kcal, this is just over a quarter of an 1,800-calorie day. It is light enough for lunch and filling enough for dinner.
