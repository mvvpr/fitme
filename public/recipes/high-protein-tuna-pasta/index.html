High protein creamy tuna pasta with peas and spinach

A tin of tuna and blended cottage cheese make this one of the highest-protein recipes in the plan: 52 g per portion, for about 540 calories. The sauce has no cream. Fold the tuna in last, in big flakes, so it doesn't break down into the sauce.

Ingredients (1 portion)

  • 110 g tuna in water (drained)
  • 60 g wholewheat pasta (dry)
  • 100 g cottage cheese
  • 60 g frozen peas
  • 40 g baby spinach
  • 1 garlic clove
  • 5 ml lemon juice
  • 1 tsp olive oil
  • Salt, black pepper, pinch of chilli flakes

Cooking for more people? Multiply everything. In your FitMe plan, the amounts are scaled to your own calories.

Method

  1. Cook the pasta according to the pack, adding the peas for the last 3 minutes. Keep a mug of the cooking water.
  2. Warm the oil in a small pan and cook the crushed garlic for 1 minute without letting it brown.
  3. Blend the cottage cheese with the garlic oil and the lemon juice until smooth, or mash it well with a fork.
  4. Drain the pasta and peas and return them to the pan off the heat. Stir in the cottage cheese, spinach and a splash of the cooking water until the spinach wilts, then fold in the drained tuna in big flakes. Season with pepper and chilli flakes.

Where it fits in your day

At about 540 kcal per portion, this is under a third of an 1,800-calorie day. It is also mostly store-cupboard food, so it is a good back-up dinner when the fridge is empty and a takeaway is tempting.
