High protein tofu and egg fried rice

Pat the tofu dry before frying, and cut it small so more of each piece turns crisp. With an egg and peas, this vegetarian fried rice gives you 40 g of protein per portion for about 655 calories. Use rice cooked the day before if you have it. It fries instead of clumping.

Ingredients (1 portion)

  • 150 g firm tofu
  • 60 g basmati rice (dry)
  • 1 egg
  • 60 g frozen peas
  • ½ carrot
  • 1 garlic clove
  • 1 tbsp reduced-salt soy sauce
  • 2 tsp olive oil
  • Spring onion (optional), ground or fresh ginger

Cooking for more people? Multiply everything. In your FitMe plan, the amounts are scaled to your own calories.

Method

  1. Cook the rice according to the pack and spread it on a plate to cool. Rice cooked the day before is even better: it fries instead of going sticky.
  2. Pat the tofu dry with kitchen paper and cut it into small cubes. Heat 1 tsp of the oil in a large frying pan or wok over a high heat and fry the tofu for 5–6 minutes, turning, until golden. Lift it onto a plate.
  3. Add the rest of the oil, the diced carrot and the peas and stir-fry for 3 minutes. Add the crushed garlic and a pinch of ginger for 30 seconds.
  4. Push everything to one side, crack in the egg and scramble it for 1 minute. Add the rice, the tofu and the soy sauce and toss for 2–3 minutes until piping hot. Top with sliced spring onion.

Where it fits in your day

At about 655 kcal per portion, this is more than a third of an 1,800-calorie day, so treat it as your main meal. It keeps well, which makes it an easy lunch box for the next day.
