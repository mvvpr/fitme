Banana protein pancakes with oats, no protein powder

Blended oats, a banana and two eggs make a batter that holds together without flour. With Greek yogurt and berries on top, you get 31 g of protein per portion and no protein powder. Keep the pancakes small, about 3 tablespoons of batter each, so they flip without breaking.

Ingredients (1 portion)

  • 50 g rolled oats
  • 1 banana
  • 2 eggs
  • 100 g greek yogurt 0%
  • 60 g mixed berries (fresh or frozen)
  • 1 tsp olive oil
  • ½ tsp baking powder, pinch of ground cinnamon

Cooking for more people? Multiply everything. In your FitMe plan, the amounts are scaled to your own calories.

Method

  1. Blend the oats into a coarse flour, then add the banana, eggs, baking powder and cinnamon and blend until smooth. No blender? Mash the banana well and whisk everything together.
  2. Heat a non-stick pan over medium heat and brush with a little of the oil.
  3. Pour in small rounds of batter (about 3 tbsp each). Cook 2 minutes until bubbles appear, flip and cook 1 minute more. Repeat with the rest.
  4. Stack the pancakes and top with the yogurt and berries.

Where it fits in your day

At about 570 kcal per portion, this is a big breakfast: close to a third of an 1,800-calorie day. It suits a morning when lunch will be late, or a day with a workout. On lighter days, a smaller lunch keeps you in your calorie deficit.
