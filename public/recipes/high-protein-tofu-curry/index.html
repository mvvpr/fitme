High protein tofu curry with chickpeas and spinach

Fry the tofu on its own first, until it is golden on several sides, and lift it out. If it goes into the sauce raw, it just turns soft and bland. With chickpeas and Greek yogurt, this vegetarian curry gives you 44 g of protein per portion for about 665 calories, rice included.

Ingredients (1 portion)

  • 150 g firm tofu
  • 80 g chickpeas (canned, drained)
  • 200 g chopped tomatoes (canned)
  • 60 g baby spinach
  • ½ onion
  • 1 garlic clove
  • 60 g greek yogurt 0%
  • 50 g basmati rice (dry)
  • 1 tsp olive oil
  • 2 tsp curry powder, fresh coriander or parsley

Cooking for more people? Multiply everything. In your FitMe plan, the amounts are scaled to your own calories.

Method

  1. Cook the rice according to the pack. Pat the tofu dry with kitchen paper and cut it into cubes.
  2. Heat the oil in a non-stick pan and fry the tofu for 5–6 minutes, turning, until golden. Lift it out onto a plate.
  3. In the same pan, cook the chopped onion for 5 minutes, then add the garlic and curry powder for 1 minute. Add the tomatoes and chickpeas and simmer for 10 minutes, then return the tofu.
  4. Stir in the spinach until wilted, take off the heat and stir in the yogurt for a creamy finish. Serve over the rice with the herbs.

Where it fits in your day

At about 665 kcal per portion, this is a big plate: more than a third of an 1,800-calorie day. Have it as your main meal, with a lighter breakfast and snack around it to stay in your calorie deficit.
