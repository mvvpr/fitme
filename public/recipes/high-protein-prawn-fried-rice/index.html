High protein prawn and egg fried rice

Cold rice is the secret. Rice cooked the day before fries into separate grains, while warm rice turns sticky. Prawns and an egg give you 46 g of protein per portion for about 570 calories.

Ingredients (1 portion)

  • 150 g raw peeled prawns
  • 60 g basmati rice (dry)
  • 1 egg
  • 60 g frozen peas
  • ½ carrot
  • 1 garlic clove
  • 1 tbsp reduced-salt soy sauce
  • 2 tsp olive oil
  • Spring onion (optional), ground or fresh ginger

Cooking for more people? Multiply everything. In your FitMe plan, the amounts are scaled to your own calories.

Method

  1. Cook the rice according to the pack and spread it on a plate to cool. Rice cooked the day before is even better: it fries instead of going sticky.
  2. Heat the oil in a large frying pan or wok over a high heat. Add the diced carrot and the peas and stir-fry for 3 minutes, then the crushed garlic and a pinch of ginger for 30 seconds.
  3. Add the prawns and cook for 2–3 minutes, until pink all the way through.
  4. Push everything to one side, crack in the egg and scramble it for 1 minute. Add the rice and the soy sauce and toss for 2–3 minutes until piping hot. Top with sliced spring onion.

Where it fits in your day

At about 570 kcal per portion, this is close to a third of an 1,800-calorie day. It is a good Friday-night swap for a takeaway that keeps you inside your calorie deficit.
