Chocolate protein yogurt with cocoa and berries

Whisk cocoa and a teaspoon of honey into Greek yogurt and it turns thick, like a mousse. That gives you 16 g of protein for about 140 calories per portion. Whisk for a full minute. Cocoa takes a while to dissolve, and lumps of dry powder are the only way to get this wrong.

Time
3 min
kcal
140
protein
16 g
carbs
18 g

2 g

per portion

Ingredients (1 portion)

  • 150 g greek yogurt 0%
  • 6 g cocoa powder
  • 1 tsp honey
  • 30 g mixed berries (fresh or frozen)

Cooking for more people? Multiply everything. In your FitMe plan, the amounts are scaled to your own calories.

Method

  1. Whisk the cocoa and honey into the yogurt until smooth and mousse-like.
  2. Top with the berries. Good as an after-dinner dessert.

Where it fits in your day

At about 140 kcal per portion, this is one of the lightest ways to end the day with something sweet. It also gets you closer to your protein target, which makes a calorie deficit easier to stick to.
