Pesto chicken traybake with roasted veg and couscous

Pesto is calorie-dense, so the trick is using less of it and putting it where you taste it: half on the chicken before roasting, half stirred through the vegetables at the end. That keeps this traybake at about 535 calories per portion with 42 g of protein. The couscous needs a kettle and five minutes, nothing else.

Ingredients (1 portion)

  • 140 g chicken breast
  • ½ courgette
  • ½ bell pepper
  • ½ red onion
  • 80 g cherry tomatoes
  • 15 g basil pesto
  • 50 g wholewheat couscous (dry)
  • 1 tsp olive oil
  • Salt and black pepper, fresh basil (optional)

Cooking for more people? Multiply everything. In your FitMe plan, the amounts are scaled to your own calories.

Method

  1. Heat the oven to 200°C (180°C fan). Cut the courgette, pepper and red onion into chunks, toss with the oil, salt and pepper on a tray and roast for 10 minutes.
  2. Cut the chicken into thick strips and coat it in half the pesto. Add it to the tray with the tomatoes and roast for 18–20 minutes, until the chicken is cooked through.
  3. Meanwhile, put the couscous in a bowl, pour over about 75 ml of boiling water, cover and leave for 5 minutes. Fluff it up with a fork.
  4. Loosen the rest of the pesto with 1 tbsp of water and stir it through the vegetables. Serve the chicken and veg over the couscous, with basil if you have it.

Where it fits in your day

At about 535 kcal per portion, this is a little under a third of an 1,800-calorie day. It is a filling main meal that leaves room for breakfast, a snack and another main while you stay in a calorie deficit.
