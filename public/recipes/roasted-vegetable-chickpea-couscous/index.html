Roasted vegetable and chickpea couscous with lemon yogurt

Dry the chickpeas well before they go on the tray, and they come out crisp at the edges instead of soft. With roasted vegetables, couscous and a lemon yogurt on top, you get 29 g of protein for about 595 calories per portion, all vegetarian.

Ingredients (1 portion)

  • 140 g chickpeas (canned, drained)
  • ½ courgette
  • ½ bell pepper
  • ½ red onion
  • 80 g cherry tomatoes
  • 100 g greek yogurt 0%
  • 10 ml lemon juice
  • 50 g wholewheat couscous (dry)
  • 2 tsp olive oil
  • 1 tsp dried oregano, 1 tsp smoked paprika, salt and black pepper

Cooking for more people? Multiply everything. In your FitMe plan, the amounts are scaled to your own calories.

Method

  1. Heat the oven to 200°C (180°C fan). Cut the courgette, pepper and red onion into chunks and toss on a tray with 1 tsp of the oil, salt and pepper. Roast for 10 minutes.
  2. Pat the chickpeas dry and toss them with the rest of the oil, the paprika and the oregano. Add them to the tray with the tomatoes and roast for 18–20 minutes, until the chickpeas are crisp at the edges.
  3. Meanwhile, put the couscous in a bowl, pour over about 75 ml of boiling water, cover and leave for 5 minutes. Fluff it up with a fork. Mix the yogurt with the lemon juice and a pinch of salt.
  4. Serve the vegetables and chickpeas over the couscous with the lemon yogurt spooned on top.

Where it fits in your day

At about 595 kcal per portion, this is around a third of an 1,800-calorie day. It is good cold too, so the leftovers make a lunch box salad the next day.
