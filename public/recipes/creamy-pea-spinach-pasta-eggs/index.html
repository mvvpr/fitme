Creamy pea and spinach pasta with boiled eggs

Two eggs boiled for 7 minutes go on top with their yolks just set, and blended cottage cheese makes the sauce creamy without cream. Together they give this vegetarian pasta 45 g of protein per portion, for about 645 calories. Cook the peas in the pasta water for the last 3 minutes. One less pan to wash.

Ingredients (1 portion)

  • 60 g wholewheat pasta (dry)
  • 2 eggs
  • 150 g cottage cheese
  • 60 g frozen peas
  • 40 g baby spinach
  • 1 garlic clove
  • 10 g vegetarian hard cheese (parmesan-style)
  • 1 tsp olive oil
  • Salt, black pepper, pinch of chilli flakes

Cooking for more people? Multiply everything. In your FitMe plan, the amounts are scaled to your own calories.

Method

  1. Boil the eggs for 7 minutes, cool them in cold water and peel. Cook the pasta according to the pack, adding the peas for the last 3 minutes. Keep a mug of the cooking water.
  2. Warm the oil in a small pan and cook the crushed garlic for 1 minute without letting it brown.
  3. Blend the cottage cheese with the garlic oil until smooth, or mash it well with a fork.
  4. Drain the pasta and peas and return them to the pan off the heat. Stir in the cottage cheese, spinach and a splash of the cooking water until the spinach wilts. Do not let it boil once the cheese is in. Top with the halved eggs, the grated cheese, pepper and chilli flakes.

Where it fits in your day

At about 645 kcal per portion, this is a big plate, more than a third of an 1,800-calorie day. Have it as your main meal, with a lighter breakfast and snack, to stay in your calorie deficit.
