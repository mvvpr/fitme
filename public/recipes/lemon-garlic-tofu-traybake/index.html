Lemon and garlic tofu traybake with potatoes and broccoli

Pat the tofu dry with kitchen paper before it goes in the marinade. Dry tofu turns golden at the edges, wet tofu just steams. One tray gives you 36 g of protein for about 545 calories per portion, potatoes included. Turn the cubes halfway so they brown on two sides.

Ingredients (1 portion)

  • 180 g firm tofu
  • 200 g baby potatoes
  • 120 g broccoli
  • 2 tsp olive oil
  • 15 ml lemon juice
  • 1 garlic clove
  • Dried oregano, salt and black pepper

Cooking for more people? Multiply everything. In your FitMe plan, the amounts are scaled to your own calories.

Method

  1. Heat the oven to 200°C (180°C fan). Halve the potatoes, toss with 1 tsp of the oil, salt and pepper and roast for 15 minutes.
  2. Pat the tofu dry with kitchen paper and cut it into thick cubes. Mix the rest of the oil with the lemon juice, crushed garlic, oregano, salt and pepper, and coat the tofu in it.
  3. Add the tofu and the broccoli florets to the tray and roast for 20–25 minutes more, turning the tofu halfway, until golden at the edges.
  4. Serve with the potatoes and broccoli. It is just as good cold for tomorrow's lunch.

Where it fits in your day

At about 545 kcal per portion, this is under a third of an 1,800-calorie day. It is just as good cold, so a double batch covers tomorrow's lunch too.
