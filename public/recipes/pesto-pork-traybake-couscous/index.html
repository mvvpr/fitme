Pesto pork traybake with roasted vegetables and couscous

Pork tenderloin is as lean as chicken breast. Cut into 2 cm medallions, it roasts in 15 to 18 minutes and stays juicy. With pesto, roasted vegetables and couscous, you get 39 g of protein for about 535 calories per portion. Check the middle: it is ready when the juices run clear.

Ingredients (1 portion)

  • 140 g pork tenderloin
  • ½ courgette
  • ½ bell pepper
  • ½ red onion
  • 80 g cherry tomatoes
  • 15 g basil pesto
  • 50 g wholewheat couscous (dry)
  • 1 tsp olive oil
  • Salt and black pepper, fresh basil (optional)

Cooking for more people? Multiply everything. In your FitMe plan, the amounts are scaled to your own calories.

Method

  1. Heat the oven to 200°C (180°C fan). Cut the courgette, pepper and red onion into chunks, toss with the oil, salt and pepper on a tray and roast for 10 minutes.
  2. Slice the pork into 2 cm medallions and coat them in half the pesto. Add them to the tray with the tomatoes and roast for 15–18 minutes, until the pork is cooked through and the juices run clear.
  3. Meanwhile, put the couscous in a bowl, pour over about 75 ml of boiling water, cover and leave for 5 minutes. Fluff it up with a fork.
  4. Loosen the rest of the pesto with 1 tbsp of water and stir it through the vegetables. Serve the pork and veg over the couscous.

Where it fits in your day

At about 535 kcal per portion, this is under a third of an 1,800-calorie day, an easy main meal inside a calorie deficit. Leftovers make a good lunch box the next day.
