High protein chicken fried rice with egg and peas

Fried rice is the takeaway most people miss when they start counting calories. This version uses two teaspoons of oil per portion, a fraction of what a takeaway uses, and the chicken and egg give you 46 g of protein per portion. Cook the rice the day before if you can. It fries properly instead of turning sticky.

Ingredients (1 portion)

  • 130 g chicken breast
  • 60 g basmati rice (dry)
  • 1 egg
  • 60 g frozen peas
  • ½ carrot
  • 1 garlic clove
  • 1 tbsp reduced-salt soy sauce
  • 2 tsp olive oil
  • Spring onion (optional), ground or fresh ginger

Cooking for more people? Multiply everything. In your FitMe plan, the amounts are scaled to your own calories.

Method

  1. Cook the rice according to the pack and spread it on a plate to cool. Rice cooked the day before is even better: it fries instead of going sticky.
  2. Dice the chicken and the carrot. Heat 1 tsp of the oil in a large frying pan or wok over a high heat and cook the chicken for 5–6 minutes until cooked through. Lift it onto a plate.
  3. Add the rest of the oil, the carrot and the peas and stir-fry for 3 minutes. Add the crushed garlic and a pinch of ginger for 30 seconds.
  4. Push everything to one side, crack in the egg and scramble it for 1 minute. Add the rice, the chicken and the soy sauce and toss for 2–3 minutes until piping hot. Top with sliced spring onion.

Where it fits in your day

At about 595 kcal per portion, this is roughly a third of an 1,800-calorie day, so it works as a main meal in a calorie deficit. Make a double batch: it reheats well for the next day's lunch.
