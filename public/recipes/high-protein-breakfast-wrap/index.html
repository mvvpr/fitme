High protein breakfast wrap with scrambled eggs and spinach

Two spoonfuls of cottage cheese whisked into the eggs are what make these scrambled eggs creamy without butter, and they add protein: 26 g per portion in total. Take the pan off the heat while the eggs still look slightly wet. They finish cooking inside the wrap.

Time
10 min
kcal

protein
26 g

per portion

Ingredients (1 portion)

  • 2 eggs
  • 60 g cottage cheese
  • 40 g baby spinach
  • 60 g cherry tomatoes
  • 1 wholemeal tortilla wrap
  • 1 tsp olive oil
  • Salt, black pepper, chilli flakes (optional)

Cooking for more people? Multiply everything. In your FitMe plan, the amounts are scaled to your own calories.

Method

  1. Whisk the eggs with the cottage cheese, a pinch of salt and pepper.
  2. Heat the oil in a non-stick pan over a medium heat, add the spinach and stir for 1 minute until wilted.
  3. Pour in the eggs and stir gently for about 2 minutes, taking the pan off the heat while they are still soft.
  4. Warm the wrap in a dry pan for 20 seconds a side. Fill it with the eggs and the halved tomatoes, add the chilli flakes, roll up tightly and cut in half.

Where it fits in your day

At about 440 kcal per portion, this wrap is roughly a quarter of an 1,800-calorie day, a solid breakfast that fits a calorie deficit and travels well.
