High protein creamy chicken pasta with peas and spinach (no cream)

The creamy sauce here is blended cottage cheese. It tastes like a light carbonara, adds protein where cream would add mostly fat, and helps this pasta reach 55 g of protein per portion. The one rule: take the pan off the heat before the cheese goes in, or it turns grainy.

Ingredients (1 portion)

  • 120 g chicken breast
  • 60 g wholewheat pasta (dry)
  • 100 g cottage cheese
  • 60 g frozen peas
  • 40 g baby spinach
  • 1 garlic clove
  • 10 g parmesan
  • 1 tsp olive oil
  • Salt, black pepper, pinch of chilli flakes

Cooking for more people? Multiply everything. In your FitMe plan, the amounts are scaled to your own calories.

Method

  1. Cook the pasta according to the pack, adding the peas for the last 3 minutes. Keep a mug of the cooking water.
  2. Meanwhile, cut the chicken into strips and cook it in the oil for 6–7 minutes until cooked through. Add the crushed garlic for the last minute.
  3. Blend the cottage cheese until smooth, or mash it well with a fork.
  4. Drain the pasta and peas and return them to the pan off the heat. Stir in the chicken, cottage cheese, spinach and a splash of the cooking water until the spinach wilts and the sauce coats the pasta. Do not let it boil once the cheese is in. Top with the parmesan, pepper and chilli flakes.

Where it fits in your day

At about 600 kcal per portion, this is a third of an 1,800-calorie day. It is the most protein of any main on FitMe, which makes it a good dinner after training.
