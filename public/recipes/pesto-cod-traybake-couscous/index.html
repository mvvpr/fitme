Pesto cod traybake with roasted vegetables and couscous

The vegetables roast first, then the cod goes on top with a spoonful of pesto and needs only 12 to 15 minutes. Cod is very lean, so this plate gives you 41 g of protein for about 505 calories per portion. Spread the pesto over the fish. It stops the top drying out.

Ingredients (1 portion)

  • 170 g cod fillet (fresh or frozen)
  • ½ courgette
  • ½ bell pepper
  • ½ red onion
  • 80 g cherry tomatoes
  • 15 g basil pesto
  • 50 g wholewheat couscous (dry)
  • 1 tsp olive oil
  • Salt and black pepper, lemon wedge (optional)

Cooking for more people? Multiply everything. In your FitMe plan, the amounts are scaled to your own calories.

Method

  1. Heat the oven to 200°C (180°C fan). Cut the courgette, pepper and red onion into chunks, toss with the oil, salt and pepper on a tray and roast for 15 minutes.
  2. Add the tomatoes to the tray, lay the cod on top and spread half the pesto over the fish. Roast for 12–15 minutes, until the cod flakes easily.
  3. Meanwhile, put the couscous in a bowl, pour over about 75 ml of boiling water, cover and leave for 5 minutes. Fluff it up with a fork.
  4. Loosen the rest of the pesto with 1 tbsp of water and stir it through the vegetables. Serve the cod and veg over the couscous.

Where it fits in your day

At about 505 kcal per portion, this is just over a quarter of an 1,800-calorie day. That leaves room for a generous breakfast and a snack while staying in your calorie deficit.
