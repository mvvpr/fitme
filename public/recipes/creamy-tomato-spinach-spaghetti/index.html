Creamy tomato and spinach spaghetti, no cream

Blended cottage cheese stirred into tomato sauce turns it pink and creamy, with no cream, and adds most of the protein: 33 g per portion, for about 530 calories. Take the pan off the heat before the cheese goes in. If it boils, the sauce turns grainy.

Ingredients (1 portion)

  • 70 g wholewheat pasta (dry)
  • 150 g cottage cheese
  • 200 g chopped tomatoes (canned)
  • 50 g baby spinach
  • 1 garlic clove
  • 10 g vegetarian hard cheese (parmesan-style)
  • 1 tsp olive oil
  • 1 tsp dried oregano, salt and black pepper

Cooking for more people? Multiply everything. In your FitMe plan, the amounts are scaled to your own calories.

Method

  1. Cook the spaghetti according to the pack and keep a cup of the cooking water.
  2. Meanwhile, heat the oil in a pan, cook the crushed garlic for 1 minute, then add the tomatoes and oregano and simmer for 8 minutes.
  3. Blend the cottage cheese until smooth (or mash it well with a fork). Take the sauce off the heat and stir in the cottage cheese and the spinach until wilted. Do not let it boil once the cheese is in.
  4. Toss the spaghetti in the sauce, loosening with a little cooking water, and top with the grated cheese.

Where it fits in your day

At about 530 kcal per portion, this vegetarian pasta is under a third of an 1,800-calorie day. A normal portion of pasta, with the protein to make it a proper meal, is a good fit for a calorie deficit.
