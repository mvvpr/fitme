High protein chicken curry with chickpeas and spinach

This is the curry for a weeknight when you want something that feels like a takeaway. Chicken and chickpeas together give you 51 g of protein per portion. Greek yogurt stirred in at the end does the job of cream.

Time
30 min
kcal
605
protein
51 g
carbs
75 g
fat
11 g

per portion

Ingredients (1 portion)

  • 130 g chicken breast
  • 80 g chickpeas (canned, drained)
  • 200 g chopped tomatoes (canned)
  • 60 g baby spinach
  • ½ onion
  • 1 garlic clove
  • 60 g greek yogurt 0%
  • 50 g basmati rice (dry)
  • 1 tsp olive oil
  • 2 tsp curry powder, fresh coriander or parsley

Cooking for more people? Multiply everything. In your FitMe plan, the amounts are scaled to your own calories.

Method

  1. Cook the rice according to the pack. Cut the chicken into bite-sized pieces.
  2. Heat the oil in a pan, cook the chopped onion for 5 minutes, then add the garlic and curry powder for 1 minute.
  3. Add the chicken and cook for 3 minutes, then add the tomatoes and chickpeas and simmer for 12 minutes until the chicken is cooked through.
  4. Stir in the spinach until wilted, take off the heat and stir in the yogurt for a creamy finish. Serve over the rice with the herbs.

Where it fits in your day

At about 605 kcal, this curry is roughly a third of an 1,800-calorie day, so it works as a main meal for most people in a calorie deficit. The protein is what keeps you full until the next meal.
