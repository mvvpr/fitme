Lemon and garlic chicken traybake with potatoes and broccoli

One tray, five main ingredients and very little washing up. You get 42 g of protein for about 465 calories, potatoes included. It is just as good cold, so it is worth making a double batch for tomorrow's lunch.

Time
40 min
kcal
465
protein
42 g
carbs
45 g

14 g

per portion

Ingredients (1 portion)

  • 150 g chicken breast
  • 200 g baby potatoes
  • 120 g broccoli
  • 2 tsp olive oil
  • 15 ml lemon juice
  • 1 garlic clove
  • Dried oregano, salt and black pepper

Cooking for more people? Multiply everything. In your FitMe plan, the amounts are scaled to your own calories.

Method

  1. Heat the oven to 200°C (180°C fan). Halve the potatoes, toss with 1 tsp of the oil, salt and pepper and roast for 15 minutes.
  2. Mix the rest of the oil with the lemon juice, crushed garlic, oregano, salt and pepper, and coat the chicken in it.
  3. Add the chicken and the broccoli florets to the tray and roast for 20–25 minutes more, until the chicken is cooked through with no pink in the middle.
  4. Rest the chicken for 3 minutes, slice it and serve with the potatoes and broccoli. It is just as good cold for tomorrow's lunch.

Where it fits in your day

At about 465 kcal, this is around a quarter of an 1,800-calorie day. That leaves plenty of room for breakfast, a snack and another main meal while staying in a deficit.
