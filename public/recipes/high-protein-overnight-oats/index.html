High protein berry overnight oats

Five minutes the night before and breakfast is waiting in the fridge. Greek yogurt brings the protein to 27 g per portion without any protein powder. Frozen berries work as well as fresh and cost less.

Time
5 min + overnight
kcal
410
protein
27 g
carbs
61 g
fat
7 g

per portion

Ingredients (1 portion)

  • 50 g rolled oats
  • 150 ml semi-skimmed milk
  • 150 g greek yogurt 0%
  • 80 g mixed berries (fresh or frozen)
  • 1 tsp honey
  • Pinch of ground cinnamon

Cooking for more people? Multiply everything. In your FitMe plan, the amounts are scaled to your own calories.

Method

  1. In a jar or bowl, stir together the oats, milk, yogurt and cinnamon.
  2. Cover and refrigerate overnight (at least 4 hours) so the oats soften.
  3. In the morning, stir, loosen with a splash of milk if it is too thick, and top with the berries and honey.

Tips

  • Forgot to make it the night before? Mix everything, microwave it for 1 minute, then leave it in the fridge for 30 minutes.

Where it fits in your day

At about 410 kcal, these oats are a little under a quarter of an 1,800-calorie day. A protein-heavy breakfast makes the gap until lunch much easier.
