Honey-soy pork stir-fry with rice

Lean pork tenderloin cut into thin strips cooks in four minutes, so this is quicker than ordering a takeaway. One teaspoon of honey and a spoon of soy sauce make the glaze, which keeps it to about 480 calories with the rice and 39 g of protein.

Ingredients (1 portion)

  • 140 g pork tenderloin
  • ½ bell pepper
  • 100 g broccoli
  • 1 garlic clove
  • 1 tbsp reduced-salt soy sauce
  • 1 tsp honey
  • 50 g basmati rice (dry)
  • 1 tsp olive oil
  • Ground or fresh ginger, chilli flakes (optional)

Cooking for more people? Multiply everything. In your FitMe plan, the amounts are scaled to your own calories.

Method

  1. Cook the rice according to the pack. Slice the pork into thin strips, the pepper into strips and the broccoli into small florets.
  2. Mix the soy sauce, honey, crushed garlic and a pinch of ginger with 2 tbsp of water.
  3. Heat the oil in a large frying pan or wok over a high heat and stir-fry the pork for 3–4 minutes until browned and cooked through. Lift it onto a plate.
  4. Stir-fry the pepper and broccoli for 3–4 minutes with a splash of water. Return the pork, pour in the sauce and toss for 1 minute until glossy. Serve over the rice.

Where it fits in your day

At about 480 kcal, this stir-fry is just over a quarter of an 1,800-calorie day. It works for lunch or dinner, and the rice can be cooked ahead.
