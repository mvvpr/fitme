Turkey meatballs with wholewheat spaghetti

Spaghetti and meatballs still fit a calorie deficit when the mince is turkey and the portion of pasta is measured. This version has 45 g of protein for about 545 calories, with spinach folded into the sauce.

Time
35 min
kcal
545
protein
45 g
carbs
54 g
fat
17 g

per portion

Ingredients (1 portion)

  • 140 g turkey mince (5% fat)
  • 200 g chopped tomatoes (canned)
  • 60 g wholewheat pasta (dry)
  • ¼ onion
  • 1 garlic clove
  • 50 g baby spinach
  • 10 g parmesan
  • 1 tsp olive oil
  • 1 tsp dried oregano, salt and black pepper

Cooking for more people? Multiply everything. In your FitMe plan, the amounts are scaled to your own calories.

Method

  1. Mix the mince with half the grated garlic, the oregano, salt and pepper, and roll into small meatballs.
  2. Heat the oil in a pan and brown the meatballs on all sides for 5 minutes. Add the chopped onion and the rest of the garlic for 2 minutes.
  3. Pour in the chopped tomatoes, cover and simmer for 12 minutes until the meatballs are cooked through. Stir in the spinach to wilt.
  4. Meanwhile, cook the spaghetti according to the pack. Serve with the meatballs and sauce, topped with parmesan.

Where it fits in your day

At about 545 kcal, this is a little under a third of an 1,800-calorie day. It is a filling dinner that does not feel like diet food.
