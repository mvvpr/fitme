High protein porridge with apple and cinnamon

Most high protein porridge recipes ask for a scoop of protein powder. This one gets to 24 g of protein per portion with milk and Greek yogurt, which you probably already have. Stir half the yogurt in off the heat so it stays creamy rather than splitting.

Time
8 min
kcal
435
protein
24 g
carbs
72 g
fat
7 g

per portion

Ingredients (1 portion)

  • 50 g rolled oats
  • 200 ml semi-skimmed milk
  • 100 g greek yogurt 0%
  • 1 apple
  • Pinch of ground cinnamon

Cooking for more people? Multiply everything. In your FitMe plan, the amounts are scaled to your own calories.

Method

  1. Put the oats, milk and cinnamon in a small pan and simmer for 4–5 minutes, stirring, until creamy. In the microwave: 2½–3 minutes, stirring halfway.
  2. Grate half the apple and dice the other half.
  3. Take the porridge off the heat and stir in half the yogurt and the grated apple. Top with the rest of the yogurt and the diced apple.

Tips

  • Swaps: Nuts are dense in calories: 10 g of walnuts is about 65 kcal. It is often worth adding more fruit instead, which is why this porridge has a whole apple and no nuts.

Where it fits in your day

At about 435 kcal per portion, this breakfast is roughly a quarter of an 1,800-calorie day. Oats and protein together keep you full through the morning, which makes the rest of a calorie deficit easier.
