Rice cakes with cottage cheese, hummus and cucumber

A thin layer of hummus under the cottage cheese gives these rice cakes flavour and stops the cheese making them soggy. You get 14 g of protein for about 210 calories per portion. Eat them straight away, though. After ten minutes they lose their crunch.

Time
3 min
kcal
210
protein
14 g
carbs

fat

per portion

Ingredients (1 portion)

  • 2 rice cakes
  • 100 g cottage cheese
  • 20 g hummus
  • 60 g cucumber
  • Black pepper, chilli flakes

Cooking for more people? Multiply everything. In your FitMe plan, the amounts are scaled to your own calories.

Method

  1. Spread the hummus thinly over the rice cakes and top with the cottage cheese.
  2. Add the sliced cucumber, black pepper and chilli flakes. Eat straight away so the rice cakes stay crisp.

Where it fits in your day

At about 210 kcal per portion, this is a savoury snack that sits easily in a calorie deficit. The protein holds you between lunch and dinner better than a bag of crisps with the same calories would.
