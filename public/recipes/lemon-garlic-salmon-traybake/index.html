Lemon and garlic salmon traybake with potatoes and broccoli

The potatoes go in first because they take longest. The salmon needs only 12 to 15 minutes on top of the broccoli. One tray gives you 36 g of protein per portion, about 575 calories, plus the omega-3 fats oily fish is known for. Take it out as soon as it flakes. Overcooked salmon turns dry and chalky.

Ingredients (1 portion)

  • 140 g salmon fillet
  • 200 g baby potatoes
  • 120 g broccoli
  • 2 tsp olive oil
  • 15 ml lemon juice
  • 1 garlic clove
  • Dried oregano, salt and black pepper

Cooking for more people? Multiply everything. In your FitMe plan, the amounts are scaled to your own calories.

Method

  1. Heat the oven to 200°C (180°C fan). Halve the potatoes, toss with 1 tsp of the oil, salt and pepper and roast for 15 minutes.
  2. Mix the rest of the oil with the lemon juice, crushed garlic, oregano, salt and pepper.
  3. Add the broccoli to the tray and lay the salmon on top. Spoon the lemon-garlic mix over the salmon and roast for 12–15 minutes, until it flakes easily.
  4. Serve with the potatoes and broccoli. It is also good cold for tomorrow's lunch.

Where it fits in your day

At about 575 kcal per portion, this is close to a third of an 1,800-calorie day, so it works as your main meal. Salmon has more fat than chicken, which is why the portion is a little smaller for similar calories.
