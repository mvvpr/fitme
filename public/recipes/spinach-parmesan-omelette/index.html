Spinach and parmesan omelette with toast

Three eggs, a handful of spinach and a little parmesan: 30 g of protein in 12 minutes. The toast keeps you going until lunch, and the whole thing uses one pan.

Time
12 min
kcal
410
protein
30 g
carbs
20 g
fat
23 g

per portion

Ingredients (1 portion)

  • 3 eggs
  • 60 g baby spinach
  • 10 g parmesan
  • 1 slice wholegrain bread
  • 1 tsp olive oil
  • Salt and black pepper

Cooking for more people? Multiply everything. In your FitMe plan, the amounts are scaled to your own calories.

Method

  1. Whisk the eggs with a pinch of salt and pepper.
  2. Heat the oil in a non-stick pan over medium heat, add the spinach and stir until wilted, about 1 minute.
  3. Pour in the eggs, tilt to spread and cook 2–3 minutes until almost set. Grate the parmesan over one half, fold and cook 1 minute more.
  4. Serve with the toasted bread.

Where it fits in your day

At about 410 kcal, this omelette is a little under a quarter of an 1,800-calorie day. It also works as a quick lunch or a light dinner.
