Basal calories (BMR) are what your body burns at complete rest, estimated from sex, age, height and weight with the Mifflin-St Jeor equation. Maintenance is BMR times your activity level. The range shows outcomes if your real daily deficit lands that many calories above or below your plan. Suggested deficits aim for a weekly loss of a share of body weight that eases with age (to protect muscle), capped at 30% of maintenance and above the minimum intake.

    This is an estimate built from the figures you entered, not medical advice. It assumes about 7,700 kcal per kg of body weight (3,500 per lb) and recalculates maintenance every day as your weight falls. Real loss is rarely a straight line: water, glycogen and metabolic adaptation make weekly numbers bounce around, and long-term loss is usually a little slower than this model shows. Once the goal is reached the projection switches to maintenance. For very low intakes or medical conditions, plan with a doctor or registered dietitian. Meal plan nutrition is estimated from standard ingredient values; portions are rounded for real kitchens, so daily totals land near, not exactly on, your target. Check labels if you have allergies.
